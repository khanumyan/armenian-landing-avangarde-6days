Questions?

Questions about the tour

What is included in the tour?

Hotel accommodation with daily breakfast, transfer to/from the hotel (airport or meeting point), entrance fees as per the itinerary and mobile internet (eSIM) for the whole trip. See the full list in the section above.

Is a guide included?

A professional guide is available as an optional paid service.

Are meals included?

Only breakfast at the hotel is included. Lunch and dinner are not included — dinners mentioned in the itinerary are optional, on request, and paid separately.

Is mobile internet included?

Yes. Mobile internet for the whole trip is included as an eSIM. Make sure your phone supports eSIM — we will send you the details to set it up.

Is the airport transfer included?

Yes. Transfer to and from the hotel — from the airport or an agreed meeting point — is included.

Are flights and visa fees included?

No. Flight tickets, visa fees and any additional personal expenses are not included.

Is travel insurance included?

No. Travel insurance is not included, so we recommend arranging it before your trip.

Is a single room possible?

Yes, with a single room supplement, which is not included in the tour price.

What should I wear?

Comfortable walking shoes and clothing for the season — in winter, warm layers, a hat and gloves. For monasteries, modest clothing with covered shoulders is appreciated.

Do ropeways and mountain activities always run?

Ropeways and some mountain activities depend on the weather and operate weather permitting.

Do I need a visa to visit Armenia?

Visa requirements depend on your nationality. Citizens of many countries can visit Armenia visa-free — see our general FAQ for details.
